# cFos striatal quantification — Fig 1 reproducibility bundle

Self-contained package to regenerate the two cFos figures for the cocaine
dose-series analysis. Everything needed is in this folder — no external
repository, notebook, or helper modules required.

Cocaine induces cFos in the striatum; this analysis quantifies cFos⁺ cell
**fraction** by striatal region (VLS, DMS) and dose (20, 30 mg/kg vs control),
and maps the **spatial density** of cFos⁺ cells per group.

## Quick start

```bash
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python run_figures.py
```

Figures are written into `figures/`. The script prints the underlying numbers
(fold-change means/SEMs, the full statistics, and the density-map pooling
composition) as it runs, and writes the statistics to `figures/fold_change_statistics.txt`.

## The two figures

**1. `fold_change_vs_control_by_region_with_SEM`** — Section-level fold change in
the cFos⁺ fraction relative to the control mean, for DMS and VLS at 20 and 30 mg/kg.
Bars = group mean fold change, whiskers = SEM, open circles = individual sections.
The fraction uses the **region-specific denominator** (cFos⁺ cells / all cells in
that region) and each region is normalised to **its own control** baseline; the
figure and the statistics below are computed from the same table, so they match
exactly. Two PDFs are produced: the native notebook size (78 × 40 mm, cropped to
content) and an exact manuscript-page size (95.955 × 89.777 mm, saved without a
tight bounding box so the PDF media box is exactly that size).

**2. `cfos_density_map_pooled_mice_and_sections`** — 2-D kernel-density estimate
of cFos⁺ cell locations, pooled across **both mice and all their sections** within
each group. Global color normalization (panels share one density scale, so control
is legitimately pale). One panel per group, titled with n mice.

## Statistics (fold-change figure)

Computed at the **section level** (as in the original analysis; n = 9 / 9 / 10
sections for control / 20 / 30 mg/kg), on the region-specific fraction with each
region normalised to its own control. The **Mann–Whitney U** family is primary
(the test originally reported); the parametric family is shown alongside.
`run_figures.py` regenerates every number below into `fold_change_statistics.txt`.

**Fold change vs control** (mean ± SEM, section-level): DMS 15.7 ± 3.4 (20 mg/kg),
23.7 ± 1.6 (30 mg/kg); VLS 7.9 ± 1.5 (20 mg/kg), 48.7 ± 7.0 (30 mg/kg).

### Induction — control vs each dose, within region

| Region | vs dose | Primary — Mann–Whitney U (FDR-BH), rank-biserial r | Secondary — Welch t, Cohen's d |
|---|---|---|---|
| DMS | 20 mg/kg | U=0, p=4.1e-4 (FDR 6.2e-4), r=1.0 | t=−4.32, p=2.4e-3, d=2.04 |
| DMS | 30 mg/kg | U=0, p=2.8e-4 (FDR 6.2e-4), r=1.0 | t=−13.43, p=1.1e-7, d=5.87 |
| VLS | 20 mg/kg | U=0, p=4.1e-4 (FDR 4.1e-4), r=1.0 | t=−4.42, p=1.9e-3, d=2.08 |
| VLS | 30 mg/kg | U=0, p=2.8e-4 (FDR 4.1e-4), r=1.0 | t=−6.85, p=7.4e-5, d=2.98 |

Omnibus: DMS Kruskal–Wallis H=18.9, p=7.7e-5 / one-way ANOVA F(2,25)=28.8, p=3.2e-7;
VLS Kruskal–Wallis H=24.0, p=6.1e-6 / ANOVA F(2,25)=35.3, p=5.2e-8.

### Regional — VLS vs DMS fold change, per dose

The expected dose-dependent regional pattern: **DMS-dominant at 20 mg/kg,
VLS-dominant at 30 mg/kg.**

| Dose | Direction | Primary — Mann–Whitney U (Bonferroni ×2) | Secondary — Student t (Bonf ×2), Cohen's d |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 mg/kg | DMS > VLS | U=16, p=0.034 (0.068) | t=−2.12, p=0.050 (0.100), d=−1.00 |
| 30 mg/kg | VLS > DMS | U=83, p=0.014 (0.028) | t=3.49, p=0.003 (0.005), d=1.56 |

The 30 mg/kg VLS>DMS difference survives Bonferroni on both tests; the 20 mg/kg
DMS>VLS difference is significant at the raw level. (These closely reproduce the
original manuscript table, e.g. 30 mg/kg t≈3.3–3.5.)

## Exclusions and data cleaning

**Section excluded (1):**

| Subject | Section | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `neg1` | `003` | **Bad-quality section** — removed before all analyses (11,455 cells dropped). |

This is the only whole **section** removed. No mice and no other sections are excluded.

**Cell-level cleaning (not section exclusions, applied to every section):**

- `fos_count > 1` → set to `0`: counts above 1 are segmentation artefacts and are
  treated as non-positive (affects 101 cells total: 100 with count 2, 1 with count 3).
- `fos_int_intensity` set to `0` where `fos_count == 0` (housekeeping; the intensity
  column is not used by either figure in this bundle).

**Region selection (not an exclusion of data, but of scope):** each section is split
into four quadrants (`vls`, `dls`, `dms`, `vms`) by its own median x/y crosshair.
Only **VLS** and **DMS** appear in the figures; **DLS** and **VMS** are computed but
not shown. Coordinates are also oriented before quadranting: y inverted per section,
and x mirrored for a fixed list of sections imaged on the mirrored side
(`c577m11`[002,004], `neg1`[004], `wt1`[004], `wt3`[005]).

## Important caveats (please read before reusing)

1. **Statistical unit is the section.** The fold-change figure and its statistics
   use the section as the unit (n = 9–10 sections/group, from 2 mice/group),
   following the original analysis. Fold change is relative to each region's own
   control mean, on the region-specific denominator.

2. **Density map pools cells raw.** A mouse (or section) with more cFos⁺ cells
   contributes proportionally more weight to its group's KDE (e.g. within the
   30 mg/kg group, wt3 contributes 6,607 cells vs c577m11's 4,284). Section and
   animal coordinate frames are overlaid **without atlas registration**, so the
   map conflates within-section spatial pattern with across-section/across-animal
   variation. Treat it as a qualitative aggregate.

3. **Region definition is a crude median-split quadrant**, not atlas-based
   parcellation.

## Provenance

- Source notebook: `notebooks/STEREO_fig1_cFosAnalysis_v2.ipynb` (STEREO project).
- Figure 1 = notebook cell "Section-level fold change with SEM", refined here to use
  the region-specific denominator (cFos⁺ / all cells in the region) rather than the
  notebook's whole-section denominator; folds shift slightly (e.g. VLS 30 mg/kg
  55→49×) but the pattern is unchanged. The statistics reproduce the notebook's
  section-level Mann–Whitney U (primary) and ANOVA/Welch-t/Cohen's-d (secondary).
- Figure 2 = the "pooled across BOTH mice AND all sections per group" cell, added
  directly below the earlier single-representative-mouse `cfos_density_map_pooled_sections`
  cell. (The earlier figure pooled sections of **one** representative mouse per
  group; this bundle's figure pools **both** mice per group — the difference is
  the whole point of Figure 2 here.)
- `run_figures.py` reproduces the preprocessing and both figures faithfully from
  the raw pickle, with no dependency on the notebook or the STEREO repo.
